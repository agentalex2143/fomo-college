FAQ

Do I need to know crypto?

No. The front-end seat is about people and events — if you can fill a room, you qualify. The back-end seat is about ops and web. The app itself does the crypto heavy lifting.

What does fomo actually cover?

Chapter events are sponsored — dinners, venue, merch. You bring the campus, the program brings the budget. Specifics get locked per chapter with the program lead.

How much time is this?

Think club-officer level: a few hours a week, spiking around events. Two seats exist so nobody runs a campus alone.

My school isn't on the board.

Apply anyway — pick "Other / my school isn't listed" on the form. The board is wave one, and strong applications put schools on wave two.

Is this affiliated with my university?

No. Chapters are student-run communities, not registered university organizations (unless a chapter registers itself). School names and colors identify campuses, nothing more.
